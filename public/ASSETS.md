# Brand assets

`jsm-logo.png` is the unmodified Juntos Somos Mais logo linked in the original challenge README:
https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c16e9208-a4ce-459c-97e9-6a9f95b2f159

Downloaded on 2026-09-28. `favicon.svg` embeds that same PNG and frames its colored emblem in a square viewport. The logo is displayed on white to preserve its original colors and legibility in all themes. These assets identify the organization whose challenge this local project implements; they are not an original logo created by the participant.
